Good to know

What does it cost?

One purchase, priced at launch. No subscription, no tiers.

What audio can I use?

Audio files you already have. Cutup does not stream and does not connect to a music service.

What is a splice?

A saved loop — start, end, speed, pitch. Opening one puts you back exactly where you were.

Where does my work live?

On your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. There is no Cutup account and no server of ours.

Can I get it all out?

Settings → Backup → Export writes a dated zip — to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, anywhere. Your tracks can travel with it or stay out of it, per export.

What happens when I import an archive?

It merges. Rows are matched on what they are, so importing the same archive twice changes nothing and importing into a library with work in it adds to it.

I have been using it on Android.

Both apps write the same archive. Export there, import here, and relink any tracks that did not travel.

Why "Cutup"?

The tape technique: cut the phrase out, play it back, play it back again. Which is what practising four hard bars is.
