Privacy Policy

Tobiano Apps built The Depths app for Android — an underwater live wallpaper. The app is free to download and customize, with an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks setting it as your live wallpaper. This Service is provided by Tobiano Apps and is intended for use as is.

The Depths is built to be privacy-first. It does not access your location, requests no runtime permissions, shows no ads, and includes no advertising identifiers, no behavioral analytics, and no crash reporting. The wallpaper is drawn entirely on your device, and your settings are stored on your device. The only permissions the app declares are the ones Google Play Billing requires — billing, internet, and network state — and they are used solely to check and process the optional purchase described below. The wallpaper itself never transmits anything.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use this app. If you choose to use this Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect personally identifiable information. It does not collect any information at all. Any settings or preferences you configure — colours, light, depth, sea floor, performance — are retained on your device and are not collected by us in any way.

Motion Sensor

If you enable the optional "tilt parallax" setting, the app reads your device's accelerometer so the scene can drift as you tilt the phone. Reading the accelerometer requires no Android permission. The readings are used only to offset what is drawn on screen at that instant; they are never stored, never combined with anything else, and never leave your device. Turning the setting off stops the app from reading the sensor at all.

Purchases

The Depths offers a single optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks setting the wallpaper. Purchases are sold and processed by Google through the Google Play Store — we never receive or store your payment information, and we do not see your name, email address, or card details. Whether you own the unlock is checked directly with Google Play on your device. We do not use any third-party subscription or entitlement service, and no pseudonymous purchase identifier is sent to us or to anyone else.
