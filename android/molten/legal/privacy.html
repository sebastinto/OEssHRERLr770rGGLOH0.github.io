Privacy Policy

Tobiano Apps built the Molten app for Android — an ambient lava lamp live wallpaper. The app is free to download and customize, with an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks additional presets and features. This Service is provided by Tobiano Apps and is intended for use as is.

Molten is built to be privacy-first. It does not access your location, requests no runtime permissions, shows no ads, and includes no advertising identifiers, no behavioral analytics and no crash reporting. The wallpaper is drawn entirely on your device, and your settings are stored on your device. The only permissions the app declares are the ones Google Play Billing requires — billing, internet, and network state — and they are used solely to check and process the optional purchase described below. The wallpaper itself never transmits anything.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use this app. If you choose to use this Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect personally identifiable information. It does not collect any information at all. Any settings or preferences you configure — presets, colours, motion, texture, performance — are retained on your device and are not collected by us in any way.

Touch

If you enable one of the optional touch settings, the wallpaper reacts to where you touch your home screen, nudging the blobs away from your finger or warming them so they rise. Receiving touches on your own wallpaper requires no Android permission. The position is used only to offset what is drawn on screen at that instant; it is never stored, never combined with anything else, and never leaves your device. Setting touch response to "None" stops the app from receiving touch events at all.

Purchases

Molten offers a single optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks additional presets and features. Purchases are sold and processed by Google through the Google Play Store — we never receive or store your payment information, and we do not see your name, email address, or card details. Whether you own the unlock is checked directly with Google Play on your device. We do not use any third-party subscription or entitlement service, and no pseudonymous purchase identifier is sent to us or to anyone else.

Settings Backup

Your wallpaper settings and any presets you save are stored on your device. If you have Android's own backup turned on, the operating system also copies them to your personal Google account so they can be restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. This is handled entirely by Android and Google — the data goes to your own account, not to us, and we cannot read it. You can turn it off in your device's system settings under Backup.

Your record of having purchased the unlock is deliberately excluded from this backup. Ownership is always re-checked with Google Play instead of being restored from a backup file.

Crash & Stability Monitoring

Molten contains none. There is no crash reporting service, no analytics, no session recording, no screenshots, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites. If the app fails on your device, nothing about that failure is reported to us.

This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing this Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your device's internal memory.

This Service does not use these “cookies” explicitly, and the app has no web content. However, the app may use third party code and libraries that use “cookies” to collect information and improve their services. You have the option to either accept or refuse these cookies and know when a cookie is being sent to your device.

One third party is involved in the app: Google, which distributes it and processes any purchase you choose to make. It is obligated not to use that information for any other purpose.

Security

We value your trust, so the app is designed so that there is nothing to lose: it holds no account, stores your settings only on your own device, and transmits nothing except the purchase check handled by Google Play.
