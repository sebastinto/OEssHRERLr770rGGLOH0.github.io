Privacy Policy

Tobiano Apps built the Bourdon app for iOS. Bourdon is a free drone and practice-tone app, with no purchases of any kind. This Service is provided by Tobiano Apps and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use this app.

The terms used in this Privacy Policy have the same meanings as in our Terms and Conditions, which is accessible at https://www.tobianoapps.com/ios/bourdon/legal/terms unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

Bourdon has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you.

Bourdon makes no network request at all. There is no networking code in the app, so there is no mechanism by which anything could reach us or anyone else. The only third party involved is Apple, who distribute it.

What Stays on Your Device

Bourdon saves your preferences so the app opens the way you left it: your selected key, drone notes, chord voicings and saved pads, along with timbre, tempo and the volume levels for tone and click.

All of it is written to the standard iOS preferences storage on your own device. It is not synced to iCloud, it is not copied to any server, and deleting the app removes it.

Microphone, Camera and Location

Bourdon does not use the microphone, the camera or location services, and will never request permission for them. It plays sound; it does not listen.

Background Audio

Bourdon can keep playing while the app is in the background, and shows what is playing in Control Center and on the Lock Screen. That information is handed to iOS on your device so it can be displayed. It is not recorded and it is not transmitted.

Analytics and Tracking

Bourdon contains no advertising, no attribution or measurement code, and no third-party tracking SDK. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not build a profile of you. It includes no analytics, and no crash or stability reporting.

Log Data

We do not collect log data ourselves. However, in the case of an error in the app, Apple and other third-party platforms (such as the App Store) may collect data and information on your device. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing this Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

Service Providers

The only third party involved in Bourdon is Apple, who distribute the app. We share your information with no one, because we hold none of it. If we engage a service provider in future, it will be listed here.

Security

We value your trust, and the strongest protection we can offer is not to hold your information at all. Your settings stay on your device, under the protection of iOS. Nothing about your use of Bourdon exists on our systems, so there is nothing there to lose.

Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age — or from anyone else. If you are a parent or guardian and you have questions about the app, please contact us at the address below.
